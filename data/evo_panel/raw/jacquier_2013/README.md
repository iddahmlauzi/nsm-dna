# Jacquier et al. 2013 — TEM-1 beta-lactamase

## Study

Jacquier et al. measured amoxicillin resistance for single-amino-acid variants of TEM-1 beta-lactamase. The reported phenotype is the minimum inhibitory concentration supported by each variant.

- Evo study: Jacquier beta-lactamase

## Sequence provenance

The FASTA contains the 861-nucleotide TEM-1 precursor coding sequence, including its terminal stop codon. Conventional TEM/Ambler numbering in the supplementary workbook differs from direct sequence indices; variants must therefore be matched through the reference residues rather than position numbers alone.

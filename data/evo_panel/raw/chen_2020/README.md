# Chen et al. 2020: VIM-2 beta-lactamase

## Study

Measured the effects of amino-acid substitutions on the antibiotic resistance, stability, translocation, and substrate specificity of VIM-2 beta-lactamase.

## Files

| File | Source and use |
|---|---|
| `A4GRB6_PSEAI_Chen_2020.csv` | ProteinGym v1.3 protein-level assay, extracted from [Zenodo record 15293562](https://zenodo.org/records/15293562). Retained for comparison with the ProteinGym benchmark. |
